    Identity

    Okta IAM & SSO rollout

    100%MFA coverage
    ↓60%Access tickets

    The challenge

    Access was spread across many separate application logins, and the audit flagged a long-standing gap in how identity was controlled.

    What I did

    • Centralized sign-in for every enterprise application with Okta SSO.
    • Enforced MFA across the whole organization.
    • Automated Joiner-Mover-Leaver workflows so access follows people as they join, move and leave.

    The result

    Every user now signs in through one secure front door. MFA coverage reached 100%, access tickets fell by 60%, and 30–50 new hires a month are onboarded automatically.

    Security

    CrowdStrike Falcon at remote branches

    100%Endpoint visibility
    −30%Security incidents

    The challenge

    Remote branches didn’t have the same endpoint visibility as head office, which left blind spots for threats.

    What I did

    • Deployed CrowdStrike Falcon to every remote branch.
    • Brought alerts into daily SOC monitoring and triage.
    • Paired detection with fast patching through NinjaOne.

    The result

    Full endpoint coverage across all sites, and security incidents down 30% while patch compliance stayed above 95%.
