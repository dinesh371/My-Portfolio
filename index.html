How I handle a cyber incident

  1. DetectAlert from EDR, firewall or a user report
  2. ContainIsolate the device, reset access, block the source
  3. InvestigateTimeline, entry point and root cause
  4. RecoverRestore from clean backups, verify, monitor
  5. PreventFix the gap and train people on what happened
